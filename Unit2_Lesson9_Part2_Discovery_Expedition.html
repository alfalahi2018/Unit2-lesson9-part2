Lesson 9, Part 2 – Literacy: Writing

Grade 7 – Unit 2: Animals – Planning a fact file and writing an information text

Welcome to the Discovery Expedition, Explorer! You have discovered unusual wild animals. Plan a fact file, write your report and check your work. Choose any channel to start – you can play the stages in any order!

👩‍🏫 Teacher's lesson plan (25 minutes)

Lesson aims: to write an information text from a fact file; to use it's and its correctly.
Teacher's Book: Lesson 9, part 2, pages 123–124 – Activity Book, Lesson 9 part 2 (exercises 1–4; Writing model on page 66). Extra: photocopiable 26.

4 minFormative 2Fact File or Text?Exercise 2 and Writing model: notes and abbreviations (fact file) vs full sentences and full words (text)
5 minFormative 3Plan the Fact FileExercise 2: plan a fact file about an unusual wild animal
5 minDifferentiationLevel UpExercise 3: Support (order the text), Challenge (write sentences from notes)
4 minClosingExit TicketExercise 4: Check your work checklist; Finishing the lesson questions

Tips: after the game, students write their own fact file and information text in the Activity Book (Exercises 2–3) and use the checklist (Exercise 4). Finish with the class vote on the strangest animal. Fast finishers list animal types (mammals, vertebrates…). Remind students about the dictation in the next lesson.
